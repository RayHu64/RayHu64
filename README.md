## Featured projects

### Sentinel: AI incident response agent
SCU Hack-A-Stack 2026 · team of 3 · won *Best Use of VoiceOS & HypeScribe*

Sorts incoming incidents by urgency with rule-based checks plus Claude Haiku (F1 0.97 on a 36-case labeled set), then pulls the right people in through chat, voice and video calls. I wrote most of the Next.js frontend and co-built the FastAPI backend.

[Repo](https://github.com/akrai37/Sentinel) · [Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/sentinel-uheo94)

### DocuAssist AI: PDF document assistant
NVIDIA hackathon, Santa Clara University

Upload a long PDF such as a grant application or compliance form and get a structured summary, extracted fields, next steps, and a follow-up chat about the document. React + TypeScript frontend, FastAPI + Pydantic backend, NVIDIA NIM models.

[Live demo](https://rayhu64.github.io/DocuAssist-AI/) · [Repo](https://github.com/RayHu64/DocuAssist-AI)

### Federated learning with FedAvg
Distributed Systems course, Santa Clara University

Federated Averaging written from scratch in PyTorch: 5 simulated clients with uneven, non-IID data. On FashionMNIST it comes within 1.3 points of centralized training (89.2% vs 90.5% test accuracy).

[Repo](https://github.com/RayHu64/Distributed-System-FedAvg)

### Speech-to-video learning assistant
Santa Clara University · 2025

Fine-tuned NVIDIA NeMo QuartzNet on 250 accented speech samples, cutting word error rate from 18.7% to 10.5%, then matched spoken keywords to YouTube lessons for English learners.
